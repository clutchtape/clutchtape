How it works

Type a gamertag. Clutchtape loads that player's recent Halo Infinite games and keeps adding new ones after each game.

What you get

  • Sessions. Games are grouped into nights, with a game log and totals for each.
  • The stats that matter. Score share, KDA, damage per minute, damage per kill + assist, objective stats, CSR change.
  • Squads. Add up to 6 players to compare them and see which lineups win.
  • Match pages. The full lobby scoreboard and a timeline of every kill, death and medal.
  • Analysis. CSR and any stat over time, every player on one chart.

With Twitch (optional)

  • Auto clips. Big moments are found in the game data and linked to that second of the stream.
  • Watch together. Every squadmate's stream of the same game, side by side and in sync.
  • Live. While you stream, the page tracks tonight's record as each game ends.

Good to know

  • Only public match data is used. You never sign in to Xbox here.
  • If your Xbox privacy settings hide your match history, we can't load it.
  • New games show up a few minutes after they end.
  • The first lookup loads your latest games, then fills in up to 200.
