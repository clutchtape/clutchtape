What we read

Only public match data and Theater films for the gamertags you type. You never sign in to Xbox and we never see a password. If your Xbox privacy settings hide your match history, we can't show it. Stats become available after games finish. Clutchtape checks for new results automatically; it does not show in-match telemetry, and new results can take a few minutes to arrive.

Where your data lives

Entering a gamertag creates or reopens a shared public stats profile. We cache the gamertag, Xbox player ID, completed-match stats and available Theater events on our server. Full match reviews can also include teammates and opponents. Anyone can look up the same gamertag or open a shared link; a lookup does not verify ownership or create a private account. We do not publish recent searches, recent visitors, or a directory of cached profiles. Demo players are fixed examples, not a record of visitor activity. Your saved shortcuts stay in your browser.

The first lookup loads up to 25 recent games first, then expands toward 200 during quieter periods. Later refreshes add new games to that history. Cached match stats expire after 180 days; non-demo profiles expire after 90 days without a visit. This is not a permanent archive. Older games outside the loaded history may be missing. Changing Xbox privacy settings later does not automatically remove cached data. Request removal and block future lookups.

Saved players, personal goals, bookmarks and private review notes stay in this browser on this device. They do not automatically follow you to another device and can be lost if you clear browser storage. Optional squad-room accounts are separate: room notes are saved on the server and restricted to that room’s members. They do not make the linked match stats private.

We also store lookup IP addresses and timestamps to limit abuse. Visitors do not supply an Xbox password. Your Xbox privacy settings govern what new data Halo allows us to retrieve.

Twitch

You don't need Twitch for stats. If you add a channel, its link travels in the page address rather than being saved to your player profile. Replays use Twitch’s embedded player. Broadcasts can expire, so not every old match has video.

Limits

We start with up to 25 recent games and gradually expand toward 200. Film reads run in small batches; opening a match review also requests its available events. We check for new games about every 90 seconds while your stats page is open (plus API and queue delays). Lookups are rate-limited per network so the demo stays fast for everyone.
