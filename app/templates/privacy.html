Privacy

Clutchtape shows public Halo Infinite match data. Here's what that means for you.

Your stats

  • Anyone who types your exact gamertag can see your stats, like on any Halo stats site.
  • There's no list of players or searches, apart from a few pro players shown as examples on the home page. Player pages aren't shown in search engines.
  • Match stats are deleted after 180 days. A player page nobody opens for 90 days is removed.

Twitch

  • If you add a Twitch channel, it's remembered only in your browser. It isn't put in links you share or shown to other visitors. If you enable streaming alerts, the channel is also stored with that device's follow settings.
  • The only channels everyone sees are the featured streamers on the home page.

Twitch videos are embedded from Twitch. Loading an embed connects your browser to Twitch and its service providers, which may use cookies, collect usage data and show advertising under Twitch’s privacy notice.

Things saved in your browser

  • Saved players and squads, player colors and your Twitch channels stay in this browser.
  • Alerts (optional): the players and Twitch channels you follow and your browser's notification address are stored on our server for that one device. Unfollow to remove them.

What we log

  • When you look up a new gamertag, your IP address and the time are kept for 24 hours to limit abuse.
  • No accounts. Clutchtape does not add its own advertising or analytics trackers. Embedded Twitch players have their own data practices, described above.

Remove your profile

We'll delete your stats and block your gamertag from being looked up again. To prove it's you, you'll be given a short code to put in your Xbox bio for a moment.
