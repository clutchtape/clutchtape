Your data, your choice.

Other visitors cannot browse your searches. Public Halo stats remain accessible by gamertag or a shared link. Saved shortcuts, gamertag colors, bookmarks, and personal notes stay in your browser. A player’s stats do not indicate that they use ClutchTape. We do not publish visitor lookup history, saved groups, follows, or color choices. Stats use a shared server cache of public Halo data; anyone can look up a gamertag, including one entered by someone else. Personal choices are not included in shared stat or moment links.

Optional alerts

If you follow players or squads under Alerts, we store those choices, alert types and quiet-hour time zone, any Twitch channels you explicitly include, and a private browser identifier on our server. Enabling phone alerts also stores your browser’s push subscription so notifications can arrive while the app is closed. Your browser’s push provider delivers them. These choices are not a public link between an Xbox player and a Twitch account. Unfollow to stop tracking a player; turn off phone alerts to delete the active push subscription. Alert history and inactive device settings expire after 90 days; rotating backups may retain them for about seven days. Installation does not enable notifications automatically.

How long we keep stats

Cached match stats expire after 180 days. Non-demo profiles expire after 90 days without a visit. You can look up a gamertag again to reload available recent history. This is a cache, not a permanent archive.

Request removal

We can remove your cached profile and prevent future lookups. To stop someone removing another player’s profile, an administrator must verify that you control the Xbox profile. After submitting, you’ll get a short code to temporarily place in your public Xbox bio. Keep the private status link to check progress. Do not send passwords or identity documents.
