ⓘ What these columns mean
KDA
Kills + assists ÷ 3 − deaths (Halo's formula), per game.
Dmg/min
Damage dealt per minute played.
Dmg/KA
Damage per kill + assist. Lower is better: less damage spent for each kill you were part of.
Dmg ±
Damage dealt minus damage taken.
SC%
Share of your team's personal score.
Dmg%
Share of your team's damage.
xK± / xD±
Kills above, and deaths below, what Halo expected for that lobby (matchmaking only).
Lobby
Your team's matchmaking rating minus the enemy's. Negative = underdog.
CSR
Ranked rating change.
Obj
The mode's objective stat: flag caps, ball time, hill time or zone caps.
Perfect/g, Multi/g…
Medals per game. Objective columns (Caps/CTF, Ball s/Odd…) average only games of that mode.

Green = best in the column, red = worst. Click any header to sort.
